DE GREAT FOOTBALL 2027 — Browser build 02

What's new:
- Five selectable fictional Uganda player profiles.
- Individual player cards with ratings, positions, shirt numbers and attributes.
- Selecting a card updates the on-pitch cartoon player shirt, number and movement speed.
- Nserekø Hamuzã retains the supplied ratings.

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or another modern browser.
3. Tap a player card to select that player.
4. Move with WASD/arrow keys or drag the player on the pitch.
5. Press SHOOT to practise shots. RESET restarts the practice setup.

This remains an early 2D browser prototype. The player figures are simple 2D sprites;
unique fully modelled 3D footballers, teams, opponents, goalkeepers and a full match
engine have not been implemented yet.
